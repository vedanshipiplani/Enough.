✦ For Parents
We know you want the best for us.
This page is for you. We're not here to point fingers — we want to share what the research says, what students are experiencing, and give you practical tools to support us in a way that actually helps.
Research shows
High pressure without emotional support increases teen anxiety and depression rates significantly
What helps
Students with parents who prioritize effort over grades report higher resilience and motivation
One shift
Replacing "what did you get?" with "how did it feel?" changes everything
Things you can try starting tonight

"Ask how school felt today — not just what happened."

"Let a bad grade pass without a lecture sometimes. We already know. We're already hard on ourselves."

"Tell us you're proud of us for something that has nothing to do with school."
